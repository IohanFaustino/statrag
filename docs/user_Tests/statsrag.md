Definition

The data generating process (DGP) is the process of realization of (time series) data.[1] Its underlying factors determine the process and are stochastic (random).[1]
Key idea

Time series data are generated through a stochastic process called the data generating process (DGP), so the data are stochastic/random in nature.[1][2] The DGP can vary over time randomly (e.g., for GDP, factors like monsoon and technology uncertainties affect the process stochastically).[1]
Why it matters

Because the data are stochastic in nature, one must examine the stochastic behavior of the variables before using them in an econometric model.[3] This is why time series econometrics focuses on the data generating process (DGP) rather than only the marginal distribution of the data.[3]
